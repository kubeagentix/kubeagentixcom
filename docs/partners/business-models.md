Build a team that creates and monetizes industry-specific packs.

| Item | Year 1 Cost | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|

| Partner Certification | $4K | Training, certification fees |
| Infrastructure | $10K | Dev/test clusters, tools |
| Marketing | $20K | Content, events, sales |
| **Total Investment** | **$154K** | |
