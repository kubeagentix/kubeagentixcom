# MSP/SI Partner Program

Build a profitable, scalable business in the AI-powered Kubernetes era with KubeAgentics.

## For Systems Integrators, MSPs, and IT Service Companies

The Kubernetes operations market is growing at 28% CAGR. However, skilled Kubernetes talent remains scarce, with 3:1 demand-to-supply ratio. **KubeAgentics enables SI/MSP partners to:**

1. **Solve the talent gap** with AI-powered automation
2. **Create recurring revenue** through Pack development and sales
3. **Differentiate services** with industry-specific solutions
4. **Scale operations** without proportional headcount increase
5. **Ensure compliance** with workspace-scoped multi-tenant architecture
